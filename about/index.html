About the Atlas

A public record of incidents affecting Catholic people, property, and places of worship.

What the Atlas counts

Each record describes one researched event. The collection includes fires, thefts, assaults, and physical damage. An event’s inclusion says who or what was affected. Its cause and any evidence of a religious motive are recorded separately.

How to read a case

The monthly map uses the documented incident date when available. Records labeled “Reported” use the report date. When the only confirmed date is a death resulting from an incident, the record identifies that date explicitly. Each case appears once.

Markers may represent the site, locality, or a wider region. The case page gives the map precision, the latest recorded assessment, and links to the sources behind it. “Undetermined” means the available evidence leaves the question open.

Coverage and sources

This edition contains 31 reviewed cases with sources or assessments reviewed through November 22, 2026. Totals describe the cases documented so far. Reporting and research coverage vary by place and month.

The Atlas updates from reviewed records in the public GitHub database. Case links stay at the same address when an assessment, location, or date changes. Basemap geography comes from Natural Earth.
