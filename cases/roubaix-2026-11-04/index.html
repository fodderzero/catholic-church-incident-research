Case record

Fire set at Catholic school entrance

FireRoubaix, FranceIncident date · Nov 4, 2026

What happened

Two people were filmed deliberately setting fire to the school entrance. The school identifies itself as Catholic. A Roubaix city councillor shared footage on November 7.

Read the Cedar News report

What the evidence shows

Published footage documents intentional fire-setting at the entrance. The source credits local reporting for the incident date. This record concerns school property.
