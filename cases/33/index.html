Case record

Fire damages Candelária parish community center in São Paulo

FireSão Paulo (Vila Maria), BrazilReported · Nov 22, 2026

What happened

Fire broke out around midnight on November 21–22 in the clothing bazaar of the parish community center in Vila Maria. Firefighters reported about 180 square meters affected and no injuries. Flames flared up again on Sunday morning. Civil Defense was called to assess the building, and Civil Police were investigating the cause.

Read the CNN Brasil report

What the evidence shows

CNN Brasil reported that Civil Police were investigating and that the cause remained unknown. The clothing bazaar was the reported area of origin.
