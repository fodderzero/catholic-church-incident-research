Case record

Parish war memorial damaged in Warrington

Physical DamageWarrington, United KingdomReported · Nov 2, 2026

What happened

Police believe the vandalism occurred on the evening of October 30. A parishioner discovered the damage on November 1. The head of the figure of Christ was broken off, and the plaque bore graffiti that the parish priest described as hostile to the Catholic Church.

Read the ITV News Granada report

What the evidence shows

The documented damage concerns the memorial on church grounds. October 30 is the police estimate for the incident date. Police announced on November 3 that they had identified a suspect.
