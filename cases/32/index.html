Case record

Virgin Mary statue found destroyed outside Knights of Columbus Hall in Petoskey

Physical DamagePetoskey, United StatesReported · Nov 8, 2026

What happened

Parishioners learned at Mass on November 8 that the statue outside the Knights of Columbus Hall had been found destroyed. The date of the damage itself is unknown. St. Francis Xavier parish leaders described it as vandalism and collected donations for a replacement.

Read the UpNorthLive (WPBN/WGTU) report

What the evidence shows

Suspected vandalism. Parish leaders described the damage as vandalism. On November 11 the Petoskey Department of Public Safety reported following leads and had yet to identify a suspect.
