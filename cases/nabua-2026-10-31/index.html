Case record

Headstones defaced in parish cemetery

Physical DamageNabua, PhilippinesReported · Oct 31, 2026

What happened

Gray paint obscured inscriptions on several headstones. Some bore obscene graffiti or an X. A family discovered the damage to their father’s headstone on October 31. Police opened an investigation November 1 and described damage at different times during October. The dates bound the reporting-supported month; the number of separate episodes is unknown.

Read the Daily Tribune report

What the evidence shows

The parish documented deliberate markings on multiple headstones. One case covers the cemetery investigation because the reports do not date individual acts or establish their number. The November 2 follow-up records a report on the eve of All Saints’ Day.
