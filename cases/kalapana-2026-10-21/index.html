Case record

Statues and crystal cross broken at Painted Church

Physical DamageKalapana, United StatesIncident date · Oct 21, 2026

What happened

Six statues were broken inside the church. A crystal cross was taken and later recovered in pieces. The historic Catholic church is decommissioned and continues to host Mass, including First Friday Mass on November 6, 2026.

Read the Hawaiʻi Police Department report

What the evidence shows

Police documented damaged statues and recovery of the stolen cross. A man was charged with fourth-degree criminal property damage and fourth-degree theft. The October 21 incident excludes window damage discovered earlier.
