Marian statue found damaged at grotto

Physical DamageKhari Bamuner Chak, IndiaReported · Nov 9, 2026

What happened

A resident discovered the damaged Marian statue on November 9 in Ghosh Para, South 24 Parganas district, at a grotto adjoining Ascension Church in the Diocese of Baruipur. Parish representatives filed a police complaint that day. UCA News placed the vandalism in the early hours of November 9, but the witness described discovering the damage afterward.

Read the Catholic Connect report

What the evidence shows

Catholic Connect reports vandalism and a formal police complaint. UCA News interviewed the parishioner who found the damaged statue and the officer leading the investigation.
