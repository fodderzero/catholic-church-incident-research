Case record

Police use tear gas to disperse Catholic marchers in Lubumbashi

Police Use Of ForceLubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the CongoIncident date · Nov 23, 2026

What happened

Police used tear gas to disperse Catholic bishops, clergy, seminarians and laypeople near Saint Paul Major Seminary. Participants had gathered and prayed as part of an archdiocesan march calling for enforcement of a court ruling concerning seminary land. Police had blocked the planned gathering at the cathedral.

Read the Agenzia Fides report

What the evidence shows

Radio Okapi and Actualite.cd reported police use of tear gas near the seminary. Fides reported the church and municipal accounts and described the march as peaceful.
