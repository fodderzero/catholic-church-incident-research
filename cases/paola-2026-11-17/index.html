Case record

Missing plaster reported around tomb slabs

Physical DamagePaola, MaltaReported · Nov 17, 2026

What happened

The Dominican friars of Birgu restricted public access after receiving reports of suspected attempts to open tombs. Health authorities confirmed that some plaster was missing around the slabs. They found no visible evidence that a slab had shifted or a tomb had been opened. The date and cause of the damage are unknown.

Read the TVMnews report

What the evidence shows

On November 18 health authorities said the missing plaster alone did not establish vandalism or attempted entry. The prior reported suspected attempts over several days, without dates for individual occurrences.
