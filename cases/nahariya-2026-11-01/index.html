Case record

Icons and statues damaged in Maronite prayer hall

Physical DamageNahariya, IsraelIncident date · Nov 1, 2026

What happened

Worshippers found icons cut and statues broken in the hall used by the Lebanese Maronite Catholic community. An apology note was left on the altar. Father Rudy Noura publicly offered forgiveness.

Read the The Jerusalem Post report

What the evidence shows

A November 11 court report corroborates deliberate vandalism and updates the evidence concerning motive.
