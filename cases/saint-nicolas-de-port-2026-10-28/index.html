Case record

Relic of St. Nicholas taken from basilica

TheftSaint-Nicolas-de-Port, FranceIncident date · Oct 28, 2026

What happened

A medallion containing a bone fragment venerated as a relic of St. Nicholas was taken. The suspect reportedly admitted burning and crushing the fragment. The parish priest filed a police complaint on November 2. L’Est Républicain and Magnum la Radio date the theft to October 28. Aleteia gives October 31.

Read the Magnum la Radio report

What the evidence shows

Local reporting describes removal of the medallion followed by deliberate destruction of the bone fragment. Both acts are counted as one incident.
