Case record

Fire closes St. Vincent de Paul Church

FireMount Vernon, United StatesIncident date · Oct 28, 2026

What happened

The parish reported no injuries. The Blessed Sacrament was transferred to the parish office chapel’s tabernacle. Extensive smoke damage closed the church. As of November 1, the pastor hoped to celebrate Christmas there again.

Read the St. John the Baptist Parish report

What the evidence shows

The pastor suspected a fan and dehumidifier malfunction and was awaiting the fire marshal’s report.
