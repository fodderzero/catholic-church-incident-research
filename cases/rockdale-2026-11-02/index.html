Crosses and statues damaged in memorial garden

Physical DamageRockdale, AustraliaIncident date · Nov 2, 2026

What happened

Wooden crosses, statuary, plants and lights in the memorial garden were damaged. Fr Yacub Barkat said the statue of Our Lady was unharmed. A man was charged with property damage.

Read the The Catholic Weekly report

What the evidence shows

The parish priest described the damage. Police confirmed destruction of items in the garden. The reported charge concerns property damage.
