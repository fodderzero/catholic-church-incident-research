Statues and candles destroyed at parish grotto

Physical DamageStaten Island, United StatesIncident date · Oct 12, 2026

What happened

Several small statues, candles and flowers were destroyed at the parish grotto. The large Our Lady of Lourdes statue was unharmed.

Read the Sanctuary Shield report

What the evidence shows

Sanctuary Shield summarizes the Staten Island Advance report. Police arrested a woman October 15. She pleaded not guilty and her attorney disputed the identification. October 24 is the public report date.
