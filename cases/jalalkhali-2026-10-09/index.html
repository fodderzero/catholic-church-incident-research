Parishioners report assault at prayer convention

AttackJalalkhali, IndiaIncident date · Oct 9, 2026

What happened

During a Santhal Christian prayer convention in Nadia district, the parish priest reported that a group entered the church grounds, beat more than ten parishioners and damaged temporary gates and protective glass around statues. Police confirmed receiving a complaint and opening an investigation.

Read the UCA News report

What the evidence shows

Two parish clergy gave published accounts of property damage. The number beaten and the attribution to Vishwa Hindu Parishad activists are the parish priest’s account. The incident date and police complaint date were both October 9. UCA News spells the village Jalalkhai.
