Our Lady of Guadalupe stained glass damaged in Stonewall parish grotto

Physical DamageStonewall, United StatesReported · Nov 20, 2026

What happened

The pastor found damage to the stained-glass image on the morning of November 20. Gillespie County investigators believe someone threw a rock at it. The parish planned repairs and re-blessed the grotto. A derogatory note was also found in the courtyard, and investigators had yet to verify a link to the damage.

Read the San Antonio Express-News report

What the evidence shows

The San Antonio Express-News quoted the sheriff on the investigation and suspected rock damage. KENS 5 reporting, summarized by LoneStar Network News, gives November 20 as the discovery date. The exact date of the damage is uncertain.
