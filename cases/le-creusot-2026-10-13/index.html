Case record

Statue of St. Joseph broken inside church

Physical DamageLe Creusot, FranceIncident date · Oct 13, 2026

What happened

Shortly before 11 a.m., a man caused a statue of Saint Joseph to fall inside the church. Its head broke off and he displayed it on a bench. Police arrested him. He later told police he had not intended to break the statue.

Read the Creusot Infos report

What the evidence shows

Both reports identify the broken statue. The November 4 follow-up records the man’s denial of intent and the prosecutor’s request for a psychiatric evaluation.
