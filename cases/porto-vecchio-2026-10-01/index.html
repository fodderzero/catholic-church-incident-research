Case record

Marian statue pulled from its pedestal

Physical DamagePorto-Vecchio, FranceIncident date · Oct 1, 2026

What happened

A statue of the Virgin Mary was pulled from its pedestal and damaged at the Stabiacciu roundabout. The statue had been installed in 2020 at the request of the local parish priest. The parish and the municipality filed complaints.

Read the AFP via Corse Net Infos report

What the evidence shows

Local reporting and video evidence documented deliberate damage to the statue. The trial report distinguishes the documented conduct from its disputed motivation.
