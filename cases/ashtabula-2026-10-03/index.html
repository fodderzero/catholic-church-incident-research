Case record

Forced entry damages former church doors

Break-InAshtabula, United StatesIncident date · Oct 3, 2026

What happened

Several people forced entry and damaged the front doors. Police reported no further damage inside. The closed building remained under Our Lady of Peace Parish while parishioners appealed to the Vatican to reopen it. The Dicastery for the Clergy later upheld the closure.

Read the Cleveland 19 (WOIO) report

What the evidence shows

Police reported charges of breaking and entering and criminal mischief. Count once as a break-in with property damage at closed parish property.
