Catholic priest assaulted at school office

AssaultKhunti, IndiaIncident date · Oct 20, 2026

What happened

Fr. Benedict Barla, a Catholic priest and principal of St. Michael Middle School, was assaulted at the school office on October 20. He was found seriously injured on October 21.

Read the The Indian Express report

What the evidence shows

The bishop’s secretary dated the attack to October 20. AsiaNews reported the discovery on October 21. The October 23 police follow-up in The Indian Express gives a conflicting incident date; it is cited for the police account of motive.
