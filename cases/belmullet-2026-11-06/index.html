Case record

Vandalism reported at convent and school chapel

Physical DamageBelmullet, IrelandReported · Nov 6, 2026

What happened

The principal of the Catholic secondary school reported extensive vandalism to the convent and its chapel. The damage was discovered and reported on November 6. Gardaí investigated criminal damage. The school appealed for information covering the preceding weeks.

Read the Western People report

What the evidence shows

One case covers the chapel and convent. An Irish Times image caption dates the vandalism to Friday, but its article dates the discovery to Friday. Gardaí confirm receipt of the report that day; the principal requested information for preceding weeks. Occurrence date left open.
