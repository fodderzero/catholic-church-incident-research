Case record

Figure of Christ stolen from academy courtyard

TheftSurrey, CanadaIncident date · Nov 11, 2026

What happened

The seated figure of Christ was stolen from the Catholic school’s courtyard installation on November 11. Surrey Police Service received the report that day. The school announced a CAD 100,000 replacement fundraising goal. Todayville described the value as an estimate.

Read the CityNews Vancouver report

What the evidence shows

School footage documented removal of the figure. The school foundation and police confirmed the theft. Damage to the installation during removal is part of the same incident.
