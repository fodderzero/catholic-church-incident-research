Case record

Restored Virgin Mary statue marked in second Porto-Vecchio incident

Physical DamagePorto-Vecchio, FranceIncident date · Nov 22, 2026

What happened

The restored statue of the Virgin Mary was marked the day after its return to the roundabout. Local reporting linked a black mark on the statue’s head to a vehicle warning triangle. Gendarmes took witness accounts and municipal workers removed the mark. This was a separate incident from the October damage recorded in Case 11.

Read the Corse Net Infos report

What the evidence shows

Corse Net Infos reported that gendarmes opened a judicial information report concerning suspected intentional minor damage. Le Parisien reported the statue was struck, while Corse Net Infos said the exact contact with the warning triangle was uncertain. The mark was cleaned off.
