Fire extensively damages St. John’s Catholic Primary School library

FireSt. John’s, Antigua and BarbudaIncident date · Nov 22, 2026

What happened

A fire reported around 9 p.m. on November 22 extensively damaged the library building on the St. John’s Catholic Primary School compound. The Royal Police Force investigated it as one of six suspicious fires reported overnight at several locations.

Read the Royal Police Force of Antigua and Barbuda via Dadli News report

What the evidence shows

The Royal Police Force of Antigua and Barbuda described the series as suspected arson. The release places the school fire at about 9 p.m. on November 22 and says the cause and circumstances remained under investigation.
