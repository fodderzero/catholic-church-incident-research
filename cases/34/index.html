Case record

St Gerard candle shrine stolen and recovered damaged in Drogheda

TheftDrogheda, IrelandIncident date · Nov 14, 2026

What happened

At about 9:30 a.m., parish CCTV recorded two people wheeling the candle shrine out of Holy Family Church. Fr Derek Ryan said the candles were scattered, the damaged shrine was recovered in a shed that morning, and an attempt to open the tabernacle had also caused damage. He said the donation box resisted attempts to open it. Gardaí confirmed they were investigating the theft.

Read the Meath Chronicle report

What the evidence shows

The parish CCTV and Garda statement support theft of the shrine. Aontú reported that cash was taken, but Fr Ryan told the Meath Chronicle the attempt failed. No cash loss is recorded as confirmed.
