Case record

Consecrated hosts and sacred vessels stolen

TheftMalali, Kaduna, NigeriaIncident date · Nov 14, 2026

What happened

On November 14 the Blessed Sacrament and sacred vessels were stolen from Christ the King Chapel in Malali, Kaduna. The tabernacle was vandalized. In a November 17 letter Archbishop Matthew Man-oso Ndagoso called for prayer, fasting and a Mass of Reparation.

Read the Archdiocese of Kaduna report

What the evidence shows

The archbishop’s signed letter dates the incident to November 14 and documents both theft of the consecrated hosts and sacred vessels and vandalism of the tabernacle as one event.
