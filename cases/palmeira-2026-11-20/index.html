Case record

Vases broken at church side altar

Physical DamagePalmeira, BrazilIncident date · Nov 20, 2026

What happened

At about 4:25 p.m., church security cameras recorded a man climbing a side altar and knocking objects to the floor. Tribuna do Paraná reported broken vases and said the Civil Police were investigating. The full extent of damage was unknown.

Read the aRede report

What the evidence shows

aRede described the incident as vandalism based on church camera footage. Civil Police were investigating the circumstances, according to Tribuna do Paraná.
