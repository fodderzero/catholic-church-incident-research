Case record

Parish priest dies after shooting

AssaultMalaguna 3, Papua New GuineaDate of death · Nov 10, 2026

What happened

Fr. Francis Paska, parish priest of St Joseph the Worker, was shot in the Malaguna 3 area on an unknown date several weeks before his death. The Archdiocese of Rabaul reported that he died from his injuries at Vunapope Hospital on November 10. The Atlas counts the case by the date of death.

Read the Agenzia Fides report

What the evidence shows

Fides cites a statement from the Archdiocese of Rabaul giving November 10 as the death date. Gaudium Press also gives November 10. FM100’s November 12 report describes the death as occurring the previous day. Neither the local report nor the archdiocesan account gives an exact shooting date.
