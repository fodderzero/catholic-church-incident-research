Saint Francisco Marto statue damaged at Wilkes-Barre grotto

Physical DamageWilkes-Barre, United StatesIncident date · Nov 21, 2026

What happened

Caretakers reported damage to the head of the statue after it fell from its limestone pedestal. They filed a police report and planned to have an artist reattach the head. How the statue came down is unknown.

Read the FOX56 (WOLF) report

What the evidence shows

Caretakers described the damage and filed a police report. FOX56 said the circumstances and whether anyone witnessed the incident were unknown.
