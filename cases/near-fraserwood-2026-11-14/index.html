Case record

Stolen church bell recovered near Fraserwood

TheftNear Fraserwood, CanadaReported · Nov 14, 2026

What happened

A 120-year-old brass bell was stolen from the church near Fraserwood. Gimli RCMP received the report on November 14 and observed damage to the bell tower. A passerby found the bell on November 17. Police reported that the bell was intact and its wooden housing had been destroyed.

Read the Manitoba RCMP report

What the evidence shows

Police confirmed the theft, observed tower damage and later reported the recovery of the intact bell with its wooden housing destroyed. The report date is used because the day of removal is unknown.
